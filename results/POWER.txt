POWER REQUIREMENT FOR A SOLVE-RATE COMPARISON

  Test                 McNemar exact, two-sided, alpha = 0.05
  Minimum discordant   6 tasks must change hands, all in one direction,
                       before any solve-rate difference can reach p < 0.05

  Discordance measured on the current 12-task sample:
    Condition               Discordant  Compared    Rate
    tail0                            2        12    0.17
    tail12                           1        12    0.08
    tail2                            3        12    0.25
    tail6                            1        12    0.08
    tailinf                          1        12    0.08

  Median discordance rate across conditions: 0.08
  Tasks required = 6 / 0.08 = 72

  A corpus of 72 tasks is the MINIMUM, and it assumes every
  discordant task falls the same way. With any task discordant in the
  opposite direction the requirement rises steeply.

  Verdict reproducibility measured across three trials: 54 of 72 cells
  agreed (75%), so single-trial verdicts carry noise of the same order as
  the effects being sought - which is why trials cannot substitute for tasks.
